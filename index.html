Clover Meadow Uploader

Clover Meadow Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Clover Meadow Friends, an original cartoon channel for children aged 4 to 7.

It uploads the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions to YouTube, schedules when they are published, and updates the channel's own banner and description. It is not offered to anyone else and does not access any other channel.
